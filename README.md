# DirectGTM-Labs

**Evidence-backed Growth & GTM engineering systems for direct opportunity acquisition.**

DirectGTM-Labs is an independent public laboratory exploring how public
signals, verifiable capability evidence, respectful direct routes,
automation, measurement, and experimentation can be combined into
repeatable Growth and Go-to-Market systems.

Our flagship project is
[DirectGTM-OS](https://github.com/DirectGTM-Labs/DirectGTM-OS).

## Core Operating Model

**Identify → Research → Detect Signals → Qualify → Engage → Automate → Measure → Learn**

This operating model treats opportunity acquisition as an engineered,
measurable system rather than a purely passive application or distribution
process.

## What Gatekeeper-Bypass Means

Gatekeeper-Bypass means reducing unnecessary dependence on low-context
screening, opaque intermediary decisions, and single-channel acquisition.

It uses:

- verifiable capability proofs;
- public and consented signals;
- qualified decision-maker research;
- multiple legitimate acquisition routes;
- respectful, low-volume engagement;
- explicit evidence and stop rules;
- measurable experiments and learning loops.

It does not mean unauthorised access, deception, fabricated evidence,
identity manipulation, data poisoning, harassment, or bypassing an explicit
refusal.

## Current Reference Use Case

The first DirectGTM-OS reference implementation is **career acquisition**.

It explores how a Growth Engineer can:

1. identify suitable roles and organisations;
2. research problems, requirements and decision contexts;
3. detect evidence-backed opportunity signals;
4. qualify fit, constraints and negative ICP conditions;
5. map requirements to verifiable capability proofs;
6. select legitimate direct and intermediary routes;
7. measure progression and outcomes;
8. turn results into reusable learning.

The underlying system is designed to generalise later to consulting,
partnership and B2B opportunity acquisition.

## Evidence Policy

Public repositories may include:

- reusable schemas and code;
- documented methodologies;
- synthetic fixtures;
- anonymised or aggregated results;
- reproducible SQL and engineering proofs;
- public-source research with clear attribution;
- explicit distinctions between fact, inference, hypothesis and unknown.

Public repositories do not contain:

- private target lists or personal contact data;
- confidential client or employer information;
- private salary limits or negotiation positions;
- unpublished outreach messages;
- credentials, tokens or infrastructure secrets;
- unsupported performance claims;
- private prompts, heuristics or proprietary operating knowledge.

LLM-generated output is never treated as evidence by itself.

## Engineering Principles

- Evidence before assertion
- Reproducibility before performance theatre
- Explicit assumptions and boundaries
- Human review for consequential decisions
- Respectful routes and enforceable stop rules
- Synthetic data before exposing private data
- Measurement before scaling automation
- Learning loops before premature optimisation

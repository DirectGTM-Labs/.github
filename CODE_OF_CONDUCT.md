# Community conduct

DirectGTM-Labs welcomes practical discussion of Growth, GTM, engineering,
measurement and opportunity acquisition.

Critique methods and claims with evidence. Explain disagreements clearly and
respectfully. Do not use harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks or spam.
Do not expose another person's private contact information, interview records,
credentials or confidential work.

Technical corrections, doubts about evidence and disagreements with project
decisions are welcome. Do not invent results or present unverified claims as
established facts.

Report a conduct concern privately to contact@mingmingcodes.com, with relevant
context and your preferred contact method. The maintainer may request changes,
remove inappropriate content, restrict participation or block an account,
according to the conduct and available context. Response is best effort.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
spaces officially maintained by DirectGTM-Labs.
